Biography of Dr. Md Motiur Rahman

Dr. Md. Motiur Rahman is an Associate Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at Pabna University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh. He obtained his Ph.D. in Civil and Environmental Engineering from Kunsan National University, Republic of Korea, where he specialized in structural dynamics, seismic risk assessment, and computational structural engineering. His research focuses on structural dynamics, seismic performance evaluation, soil–structure interaction, structural health monitoring, and vibration control of civil infrastructure systems. He has extensive experience in advanced numerical modeling using OpenSees, ABAQUS, ANSYS, LS-DYNA, and other high-performance computational tools. Dr. Rahman has contributed to numerous international peer-reviewed journals and conference proceedings, particularly in the fields of earthquake engineering, nuclear power plant structural safety, dam safety, and structural resilience under extreme loading conditions. He has also been involved in several international research projects funded by organizations in South Korea and Bangladesh. He is actively engaged in teaching undergraduate and graduate courses, supervising research students, and developing innovative computational approaches for solving complex structural engineering problems. His long-term research vision is to develop resilient, intelligent, and sustainable structural systems for disaster-prone regions.

About S3RG

The Smart Structural Systems Research Group (S³RG) is an advanced research group focused on the development of intelligent, resilient, and computationally efficient structural engineering solutions for modern infrastructure systems. The group specializes in structural dynamics, seismic risk analysis, vibration control, soil–structure interaction, and structural health monitoring, integrating traditional civil engineering principles with modern computational methods, including finite element modeling, machine learning, and digital simulation tools. S³RG aims to address critical challenges in infrastructure safety and sustainability, particularly under extreme loading conditions such as earthquakes, wind, and progressive deterioration. The group actively explores next-generation approaches such as digital twin modeling, smart sensing systems, and data-driven structural analysis. The research group is committed to producing high-impact scientific publications, fostering international collaboration, and training the next generation of engineers and researchers in advanced structural engineering.
